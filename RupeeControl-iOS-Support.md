## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
Only on your iPhone, encrypted at rest by iOS. There is no account, no server,
and no sync. We cannot see your data.

**How do I move my data to a new phone?**
More → Backup & Restore → create a backup, save it somewhere you can reach from
the new phone (iCloud Drive or Files), then restore it there.

**Can I move a backup between Android and iPhone?**
Yes. The backup format is shared between both versions of Rupee Control.

**Why can't the app read my bank SMS automatically?**
iOS does not allow any app to read your messages or other apps' notifications.
Instead, share a bank SMS or UPI receipt into Rupee Control from the share sheet
and it will read the amount for you. See **More → Smart Expense Auto-Tracker**.

**I bought Pro but still see ads.**
Open **More → Upgrade to Pro → Restore Purchases**. Purchases are tied to the
Apple ID that bought them, so make sure you are signed in with the same one.

**How do I remove ads?**
The one-time **Rupee Control Pro** purchase removes all ads permanently and
unlocks Excel and CSV export. PDF export is always free.

**I lost my data.**
If you deleted the app, its local data went with it — that is how iOS works, and
it is also why nothing of yours ever sits on a server. Restore from a backup file
if you made one.
